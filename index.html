Free & open · Single file · Zero setup

Qualitative analysis, unplugged.

QualiApp is a full qualitative research studio — codebooks, coded transcripts, themes, memos, and intercoder reliability — that lives entirely in one HTML file on your own computer. Open it, and start making sense of your data. No sign-up, no subscription, no server, no internet required.

Download QualiApp Try it in this browser
One file, ~700 KB. Works the moment it finishes downloading.
Built for laptop and desktop browsers. QualiApp is a heavy-duty research tool with dense tables, drag-and-drop coding, and multi-panel views — it is not designed for phones or tablets. Once downloaded, it runs completely offline: no internet connection is needed to open, code, or analyze your projects, and your data never leaves your machine.

Everything a qualitative researcher reaches for

Built around the actual workflow of coding, theming, and making claims from data — not a generic notes app wearing a research costume.

Codebook Builder

Grow your codebook inductively or deductively, organize codes into hierarchies, and keep definitions consistent across every transcript.

Transcript Coding

Highlight, tag, and annotate interview and focus group transcripts directly in the browser, with adjustable text size for long coding sessions.

Themes & Memos

Cluster codes into emerging themes and capture analytic memos and reflexivity notes right alongside the data that inspired them.

Intercoder Reliability

Export intercoder assignments, bring back independent coding, and check agreement to strengthen the trustworthiness of your findings.

Fieldnotes & Observations

Log observations alongside interviews so your codebook stays grounded in everything you saw and heard in the field, not just what was said.

Password-Protected Backups

Export an encrypted project backup to move between devices or hand off to a collaborator — your raw data stays under your control, always.
