Free & open · Single file · Zero setup

Qualitative analysis, unplugged.

A full qualitative research studio in one HTML file — codebooks, coded transcripts, themes, and intercoder reliability. No sign-up, no server, no internet required.

Download QualiApp Try it in this browser
One file, ~5.9 MB. Works the moment it finishes downloading.
  • Desktop & laptop browsers
  • Completely offline — your data never leaves your machine
  • Supports your thinking; never generates codes or themes

Built for how you actually analyze

Codebook & Theme Builder

Cluster codes into initial themes, then overarching themes.

Transcript Coding

Highlight, tag and annotate, with a reflexive journal alongside.

Realist Themes & 4 Cs

Two ready-made alternatives to reflexive theming.

Intercoder Reliability

Send out assignments, bring back coding, check agreement.

Fieldnotes & Observations

Keep what you saw alongside what was said.

Encrypted Backups

Move between devices or share with a collaborator, password-protected.

Grounded in published methods

Reflexive Thematic Analysis

Braun, V., & Clarke, V. (2022). Thematic analysis: A practical guide (A. Maher, Ed.).

Realist Thematic Analysis

Wiltshire, G., & Ronkainen, N. (2021). A realist approach to thematic analysis: Making sense of qualitative data through experiential, inferential and dispositional themes. Journal of Critical Realism, 20(2), 159–180.

The 4 Cs Model

Christodoulou, M. (2024). The four C's model of thematic analysis: A critical realist perspective. Journal of Critical Realism, 23(1), 33–52.
