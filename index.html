How it works

  1. 1Generate. Create each reference from a link, DOI or PDF and review the flagged fields.
  2. 2Collect. Add citations to the bibliography. It sorts them and fixes common APA slips.
  3. 3Export. Copy with formatting or download a Word file. Tables work the same way.

Your data stays on your device. Papers, tables, bibliographies and contents pages are processed in your browser and never uploaded. The only exception is the Citation Generator, which sends the link or DOI you enter to public lookup services (such as Crossref) to find its details. A saved bibliography draft is kept in this browser only, and you can discard it from the home page.
