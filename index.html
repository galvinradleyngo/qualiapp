Free & open · Single file · Zero setup

Qualitative analysis, unplugged.

QualiApp is a full qualitative research studio — codebooks, coded transcripts, themes, memos, and intercoder reliability — built around Reflexive Thematic Analysis, Realist Thematic Analysis, and the 4 Cs Model. It lives entirely in one HTML file on your own computer. Open it, and start making sense of your data. No sign-up, no subscription, no server, no internet required.

Download QualiApp Try it in this browser
One file, ~700 KB. Works the moment it finishes downloading.
Built for laptop and desktop browsers. QualiApp is a heavy-duty research tool with dense tables, drag-and-drop coding, and multi-panel views — it is not designed for phones or tablets. Once downloaded, it runs completely offline: no internet connection is needed to open, code, or analyze your projects, and your data never leaves your machine.

Grounded in the methods, not just the metaphor

QualiApp's workflows follow published qualitative methods, so your process holds up to methodological scrutiny.

Reflexive Thematic Analysis

Inductive coding, researcher reflexivity, and theme development as an active, interpretive process rather than a fixed procedure.

Braun, V., & Clarke, V. (2022). Thematic analysis: A practical guide (A. Maher, Ed.).

Realist Thematic Analysis

An alternative to reflexive TA that builds experiential, inferential, and dispositional themes from a critical realist standpoint.

Wiltshire, G., & Ronkainen, N. (2021). A realist approach to thematic analysis: Making sense of qualitative data through experiential, inferential and dispositional themes. Journal of Critical Realism, 20(2), 159–180.

The 4 Cs Model

A critical realist framework for connecting codes and categories through causes, contexts, consequences, and contingencies.

Christodoulou, M. (2024). The four C's model of thematic analysis: A critical realist perspective. Journal of Critical Realism, 23(1), 33–52.

🎓 Sample project: "Adjusting to first-year college life"

Three interview transcripts, coded end to end with Reflexive Thematic Analysis:

  • 3 transcripts — interviews with first-year students about their first semester.
  • 6 codes applied across 15 excerpts (e.g. Homesickness, Peer support networks, Growing academic confidence).
  • A full analysis workspace — the codes clustered into 3 initial themes and 1 overarching theme, "Becoming an independent learner."
Open the sample project

Opens QualiApp in a new tab with the sample project already loaded — no import steps. Go to Reflexive TA in the sidebar and choose Initial Themes from the "Switch" workspace dropdown to see the finished analysis.

Prefer to import it yourself instead? Download the .qbk2 file and use Import a backup… on the projects screen with the password sample123.
